Zoom Developer Ecosystem & Zoom Apps

Driving and hands-on in the design of Zoom’s Developer Ecosystem, including in-house apps like Timer, Music, Virtual Backgrounds, as well as the Developer App Builder and Marketplace that empower third-party developers to create and launch their own Zoom apps. These experiences expand collaboration, engagement, and personalization across the Zoom platform.

Zoom Timer App
The #1 most popular app in the Zoom Marketplace, this user-favorite helps millions of users daily manage and optimize meeting time with customizable countdowns, intuitive controls, and real-time notifications. Designed to boost productivity and keep meetings on track, it’s trusted by professionals and educators worldwide.

Zoom Virtual Background App
Transforms video meetings with stunning, high-quality backgrounds and advanced AI-powered customization, allowing users to generate unique, professional, and creative virtual environments. We also integrated an AI virtual background option, delivering dynamic, adaptive backgrounds that enhance engagement and visual appeal for millions of users.

Music App
Enhances meetings and brainstorming sessions by creating an inviting, energizing atmosphere that sparks collaboration, creativity, and focus. Trusted by professionals and teams worldwide, it transforms ordinary calls into engaging, productive experiences.

Zoom App Builder
Empowers developers to create custom apps that integrate seamlessly into Zoom surfaces like Meetings, Chat, and Webinars, enhancing user experiences and unlocking new productivity and collaboration possibilities. Trusted by developers worldwide to extend Zoom’s capabilities.
