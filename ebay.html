Shutterfly

A web-based photo service, where you can make your photos more creative, personal, and fun.

Create Calendar Tool

This project is to create an easy and intuitive experience for users to create a calendar product. This design contains a guided step to lead the users through the process of creating and customizing their calendar with their personal photos and events.

My Pictures

This page allows users to easily view, organize, and select photos to help them quickly create a product, share a product or photos with their friends. This design offers the users a better picture management experience with new and improved selection and organizational tools to manage their photo collection.
