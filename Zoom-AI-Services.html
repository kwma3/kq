 Zoom AI Services

Architecting Zoom’s Unified AI Developer Platform
As Lead Designer and Architect, I defined the product strategy and experience foundation for Zoom AI Services, transforming proprietary speech, vision, and language intelligence into self-serve developer APIs. Directing product experience from zero to GA in 8 weeks across 4 engineering squads, the platform unlocked a $50M enterprise pipeline and established a high-growth commercial revenue stream.

The Universal Playground Framework
A unified split-pane interface: interactive inputs and live visual outputs on the left, synchronized code generation and parameter controls on the right. Standardized across Speech, Audio, Translation, and Vision.

Observability: Tracing & Usage
Full transparency for production reliability: real-time latency waterfall traces, error diagnostics, per-endpoint usage breakdowns, and proactive credit burn monitoring.

The 4-Stage Self-Serve Journey
A friction-free funnel taking evaluators from discovery to production-scale integration:

  • 01. Explore: Zero-barrier sandbox letting evaluators run live models instantly—no login or credit card required.

  • 02. Onboard: 1-click API key generation that carries active playground parameters straight into authentication.

  • 03. Integrate: Production-ready snippets (cURL, Python, Node.js) driving time-to-first-API-call down to minutes.

  • 04. Monitor: Live tracing, latency histograms, credit consumption, and automated threshold alerts.

The Challenge: Speed, Scale & Opposing Audiences
Balancing dual evaluator personas while shipping on an unyielding 8-week launch runway:

  • Dual Evaluator Tension: Technical buyers need instant code snippets, cURL parity, and API docs; business decision-makers need interactive playgrounds with live sample media to validate accuracy before committing budget.

  • Fragmented Discovery: Zoom’s AI APIs were scattered across siloed documentation without a unified onboarding path or self-serve trial engine.

  • Moving Technical Foundation: Designed core flows while backend architecture and API endpoints were actively evolving in parallel.

Business & Strategic Impact
Accelerating go-to-market velocity while establishing company-wide platform standards:

  • First-Pass Executive Approval: Secured immediate green-lights from CEO and CTO by anchoring designs in technical feasibility and enterprise ROI.

  • Keynote Showcase: Delivered the production-grade live playgrounds featured in the CTO’s stage keynotes at Zoomtopia and HumanX.

  • Ecosystem Standard: The playground design patterns and documentation hierarchy were adopted as the foundation for all developer tools across Zoom.

  • High-Velocity Trial Engine: Reduced evaluator friction to under 60 seconds, driving immediate enterprise adoption upon General Availability.
