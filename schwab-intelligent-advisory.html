Schwab Intelligent Advisory

This hybrid advisory application offers a self-guided planning process, scheduling the virtual meeting with an advisor, signing up, and monitoring. When a prospects opens an advised accounts, they can manage their financial plan, track progress toward goals, and monitor portfolio performance.

How it works:
1. User sets the goals through the MoneyGuide Pro tool.
2. Use online scheduler to setup a virtual appointment with a planning consultant.
3. Collaborate with the planning consultant to discuss the user’s goals and plan options.
4. Activate the plan and fund the account.

This application provides investors with a financial plan tailored to their goals.

Clients have access to live credentialed planning consultants to help create an investment portfolio that is aligned with the client’s risk tolerance and goals.
