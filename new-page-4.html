 Zoom AI Companion

As Zoom’s flagship AI companion, our vision is to create an intelligent, centralized destination where enterprise data is transformed into actionable momentum. Designed to move beyond the meeting, it empowers users with a “second brain” that orchestrates knowledge across their entire ecosystem, driving smarter decisions and more seamless collaboration.

Target Audience
During the discovery phase, we worked to deeply understand our target users, their needs, behaviors, and goals, by identifying clear patterns through focused research. We defined three core user segments, each with distinct challenges and expectations, and mapped their workflows to uncover key friction points. This ensured the AI Companion was intentionally designed to solve meaningful, high impact problems while giving us a strong user centered foundation to guide product decisions and feature prioritization.

Challenge: The “Intelligence Gap”
Our research revealed that the problem isn’t a lack of data, it’s fragmented and siloed, blocking productivity and decision-making.

  • Data Silos: Insights are trapped in Zoom transcripts, Slack threads, or Google Drive, there’s no single source of truth.

  • Context Switching: Users spend time hunting across tools, creating cognitive overload.

  • Static AI: Competitors deliver generic outputs because they lack your team’s data. We focus on what your team actually decided.

Guiding Principles & KPIs
If an AI suggestion does not save time, drive action, or earn trust, it does not belong in Zoom AIC. To hold ourselves accountable, we defined three measurable KPIs:

  • Impact: measured by the percentage of users completing tasks faster than before.

  • Actionable: tracked by how often users act on an AI suggestion, proving it moves them from insight to execution.

  • Personal and Trust: evaluated through user satisfaction and trust scores to ensure the experience is context aware and reliable.

Solution
Zoom AIC is fully integrated into the enterprise ecosystem, combining intelligence, action, and security in one seamless experience. Our solution is built on three pillars:

  • Holistic Data Orchestration: connects Zoom data with external tools, eliminating fragmented information and answering complex questions.

  • Agentic Action: lets AI handle chains of tasks, like preparing for a meeting and drafting follow-up emails, moving beyond basic suggestions to real execution.

  • Privacy-First Intelligence: keeps all data secure, giving enterprises confidence to deploy at scale.

AI Companion Impact
The AI Companion empowers users by handling the heavy lifting, letting them focus on critical work. Key impacts include:

  • Operational Velocity: unifying fragmented data drives a 35% increase in execution speed, giving users more time to act and deliver results.

  • Actionable Intelligence: goes beyond summaries to turn insights into real work, from drafting complex deliverables to closing the loop on sales proposals or product roadmaps.

  • Trusted and Secure: personalized, reliable, and privacy-first intelligence ensures all data stays safe while driving measurable impact.
